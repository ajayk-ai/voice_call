How the escalation works

Every PO line with OD Days > 0 is pending. It moves one step at a time; each step waits for the interval set in Settings. A line that is no longer pending is marked CLEARED and stops. The numbers come from the Phone_Master tab (Scenario 1 / 2 / 3), falling back to Master.

      Test the whole escalation on one vendor

      1. Pick a vendor that has pending lines in the test data.
      2. Enter your WhatsApp number. Every message goes to you (pilot mode), starting with a line that says who would really get it.
      3. Choose the wait between scenarios (1 minute instead of 1 day) and press Start test.

      Scenario 1 is sent right away. Then, one wait apart: Scenario 2, Scenario 3 (buyer call task) and the Day 3 meeting, so the full flow finishes in about 4 waits. Starting resets this vendor's test history and changes the test settings (pilot mode on, this vendor only, your number, the wait).
